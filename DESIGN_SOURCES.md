The first direction is a dark desktop/mobile web simulation: cyan/blue accent, conversation-first center, slim functional navigation, list/card outputs and restrained dark surfaces. Card colors #14181E/#1B2028 and action colors #2D415E/#232F40 are documented. Sans-serif text, generous spacing and labels draw from the guide. Minimum 48px interactive target height and visible focus ring are implemented.

We use original CSS, a QuietWindow wordmark and native text symbols. No Amazon logo, Amazon proprietary font, official screenshots or marketing assets are bundled, and no endorsement is claimed. It is a web simulation, not a certified Alexa add-on or a device-tested MCP Apps interface. Dark-first preview only; light mode, full contrast audit and on-device assistive technology testing remain future work.
